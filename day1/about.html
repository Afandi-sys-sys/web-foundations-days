About QuickNotes

QuickNotes ia a note-taking tool. It is user friendly to assist the user to create and manage not forgetting organize notes.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open your QuickNotes.
  2. Create a new note.
  3. Take/write your notes and save them.
